Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Technology Adoption among Healthcare Professionals in Bangladesh: Evidence from PLS-SEM

Sole author. American Journal of Smart Technology and Solutions, 5(2). E-Palli Publishers.

A survey of 460 healthcare professionals, analysed with PLS-SEM, on what makes AI adoption more likely: literacy, organizational support, infrastructure, usefulness and perceived risk. The model explains 45% of the variance in AI readiness and 43% in reported adoption.

A CNN-Transformer Encoder-Decoder for Image Captioning: Label Smoothing, Backbone Fine-Tuning, and Length-Normalized Decoding

With eight co-authors. Research Square, not yet peer reviewed.

A small Transformer captioner trained on MS COCO. Ablations show decoding strategy matters most, and a BatchNorm-freezing mistake limited the fine-tuning gains.
